Rules for Fair & Safe Golfing

  1. Tee Off: All players in the group hit their 1st shot, then finish the hole in rotation.
  2. Group Size & Stroke Limit: Maximum 4 players. Stroke limit is 6 shots per hole, per person.
  3. Out of Bounds: Place ball back in play 1 club head's length from where it went out. +1 penalty stroke.
  4. Obstacles: If a side obstacle or rail prevents putting, the ball may be moved one club head's length away. No penalty stroke.
  5. Clubs: Never swing your club above knee level.
  6. Safety: Use the walkways at all times. Do not walk on bricks. Do not run.
  7. Courtesy: Play the course all the way through. Do not skip around the course.
  8. Trash: Please help keep our park clean! Use the trash containers.
