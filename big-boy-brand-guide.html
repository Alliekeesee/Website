Big Boy Wisconsin Dells

Brand Guide & Client Reference

Onboarded April 2026
1. Core Brand Identity
Brand Mission
Bring back the memories of WI Big Boy from 30 years ago — and create new ones.
Brand Story
Big Boy was brought back to Wisconsin after a 28-year absence. Started in metro Milwaukee, then moved to Wisconsin Dells in 2024 — now the only Big Boy location in the state. (Reference: "Mashed — The Untold Truth of Big Boy" on YouTube for brand background.)
Core Values
1. Nostalgia — bring back memories & make new ones
2. Burger quality & consistency (Big Boy burger & others)
3. Family friendly — great for all ages, so much to look at
Brand Personality
Fun Nostalgic Family
Note: They own a Big Boy mascot outfit — great for video content.
Tone of Voice
Friendly & Playful — nostalgic feel with current cool slang mixed in.
Emoji Usage
NO emojis.
2. Target Audience & Ideal Customer
Primary Audience
Families with Kids Burger Lovers
Demographics
Tourists, mixed genders & income levels.
Customer Interests
Entertainment; great place for social activities and social media content.
Customer Pain Points
Bored with the same spots — craving something unique and memorable.
Who is NOT the Customer
Health-conscious diners or those with dietary restrictions.

5. Brand Boundaries & Guidelines
Topics to Avoid
Politics and all controversial issues — avoid all of the above.
Language & Tone
Nostalgic base tone with current cool slang mixed in. Keep it fun and approachable.
Content Approval
Month 1: Send all drafts for approval.
After Month 1: Follow guidelines independently.
Response Protocol
Flag all comments/DMs to client — they handle customer service directly.
Big Opportunity: Big Boy Wisconsin Dells has a huge collection of visuals — the mascot outfit, statues, and food photography — that positions this brand to become the coolest, most shareable restaurant social presence in the Dells. Lean into the visual assets hard.
